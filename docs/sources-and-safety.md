# Sources and safety

Cat Compass is an owner guide and record tool. It is not a diagnosis service. A veterinarian has not reviewed or approved the whole app.

## Emergencies

Contact a veterinarian immediately for trouble breathing, little or no urine, collapse, possible poisoning or sudden painful weakness in the back legs. Do not wait for an app reminder or a GitHub reply.

Contact your vet promptly when a cat stops eating. For an adult, 24 hours is a risk threshold, not an instruction to wait. Kittens and ill cats need advice sooner. Do not force food into the cat or induce vomiting unless your vet directs you.

The app links exact Cornell, RSPCA and other source pages beside warning signs. The [Australian Animal Poisons Helpline](https://www.animalpoisons.com.au/) lists **1300 869 738**. Its current page states fees apply. Service terms and numbers can change.

## How sources are labelled

Each of the 31 records includes a publisher, exact URL, checked date, read section and limits.

| Evidence type | Use |
|---|---|
| Veterinary guideline | Formula, vaccine distinctions and care principles |
| Peer-reviewed study or review | Specific findings with population and study limits |
| University veterinary education | Owner warning signs and disease background |
| Welfare organisation | Safety and practical care guidance |
| Veterinary clinic opinion | Local or dated advice, not a national schedule |
| Secondary owner guide | Further reading, not the calculator authority |
| Official government page | Local rules, with unresolved boundaries stated |

Read dates are **13 October 2026**. Dates do not mean a source has remained unchanged. [The research ledger](../evidence/veterinary-research.json) retains excerpts, locations, access failures and recommended corrections.

## Feeding boundary

`RER = 70 × weightKg^0.75` and the feline factors follow AAHA 2021 Box 1. Kilograms are required. The app maps normal/high activity to the low/high ends of adult ranges; AAHA does not define that mapping.

Treats are at most 10% of total daily calories. Their calories are deducted before portion conversion. The weight loss range comes from a separate source, not Box 1. The app does not forecast an end date or turn a body condition score into a target weight.

The app rejects unweaned kittens. Illness, low body condition, pregnancy and nursing require a daily target set by your vet. No formula can verify that a manually entered target really came from a vet.

## Scope and unresolved points

- No medication dose, homemade diet formulation, milk feed plan for newborn kittens or symptom diagnosis is supplied.
- Breed notes describe tendencies, not diagnoses. The list is not complete. A negative genetic test does not rule out HCM.
- Vaccine timing depends on history, product and exposure. FPV protection differs from FHV/FCV protection.
- Parasite risk depends on habitat and travel. The app does not assume any region is free of ticks.
- Guidance is written mainly for Australia. Check local laws and product labels elsewhere.
- The exact ACT containment boundary for a cat born on 1 July 2022 needs current statutory or Domestic Animal Services confirmation.
- Fixed play durations, training steps and shopping costs are practical examples, not clinically validated targets.

## Content corrections

Open a [content correction issue](https://github.com/Guinerve/cat-compass/issues/new/choose). Give the exact claim, a direct source URL, a section or page number and the proposed correction. State source limits. Do not use search snippets as proof of clinical agreement.

Potentially dangerous guidance is corrected or withheld while the source is checked. Do not post private records. Clinical review must name its scope and date; a review of one formula is not approval of the whole app.

Cats.com is further reading. Its feeding-amount article differs from AAHA and is not used as the formula authority. Its text, photographs and application code are not republished in the product. [Evidence notes](../evidence/README.md).
