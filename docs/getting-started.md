# Your first care plan

Keep the cat's real details and vet instructions nearby. New profiles contain examples. Do not treat those values as facts about your cat.

## Profile

1. Open **Profile**. Enter the name, age in whole months, breed and current weight in kilograms.
2. Set sex, desexing status, lifestyle and region.
3. Use the linked vet chart for body condition. Ask your vet to confirm the score if you are unsure.
4. Record known health conditions, vet contacts and a microchip number if available.
5. Confirm that age, weight and body condition are real values, not examples.

Use **Add a cat** for another companion. Records, reminders and checklists are separate for each cat. Removing a cat also removes their related records after confirmation.

## Feeding

1. Find the metabolisable energy on each food label.
2. For dry food, enter **kcal/kg**. Multiply kcal/100 g by 10. Divide kJ/kg by 4.184.
3. For wet food, enter **kcal per whole can or pouch** and its weight in grams. For kcal/100 g, multiply by pack grams divided by 100.
4. Choose the wet share of calories, not the wet share of grams.
5. Enter a whole number of meals and all treat calories.
6. Confirm the label values and that the food is complete for this cat's life stage.

The result is a starting estimate. Treat calories come out of the daily allowance. Do not add the treat allowance on top of the food total.

Illness, low body condition, pregnancy and nursing require a calorie target set by your vet. Weight loss also needs a target weight agreed with your vet, below the current weight, and an overweight body condition score. Do not restrict a kitten, fast a cat or invent a target.

## Daily care and records

Use **Today** for water, litter, play and comfort checks. The daily checklist resets with the local calendar date. The New owner checklist keeps its progress.

Add dated observations, vet visits and weight checks in **Care journal**. A weight check updates the current profile weight only if you select that option. The chart shows records, not a diagnosis.

Use **Health** for questions to discuss with your vet. It does not calculate medical due dates. Enter only agreed dates in **Reminders**.

## Before you leave the app

Export a full backup from **Phone & backup**. Store it privately. Print the **Care sheet** for a sitter or vet. The sheet omits feeding amounts if profile or food details are invalid or unconfirmed.
