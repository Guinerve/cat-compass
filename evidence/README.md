# Cat Compass evidence

Sources were read on 13 October 2026. This folder records what was checked, what the app assumes and what is still unknown. It is not a sign off by a vet.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `veterinary-research.json` | 27 sources with excerpts, section locations, limits and the corrections they led to |
| `release-0.2.0.json` | Build runs, checksums and the checks run on the 0.2.0 release |

The app adds 5 more records to the ledger: the WSAVA body condition toolkit page, Cornell dental guidance, two Cats.com practical guides and the Animal Poisons Helpline. It leaves out one Cats.com feeding amount article, because its numbers do not match AAHA. That gives the 31 records on the Sources page.

## How the sources are used

Guideline sections support the energy factors, the vaccine distinctions and the care principles. Clinic advice on parasites is labelled as local opinion with a date. The ledger keeps access failures and the legal or clinical points that are still open.

The source checks led to these corrections:

- Breathing trouble and suspected permethrin exposure now mean act at once.
- The adult F3 text no longer applies one 3 year rule to every vaccine part and every level of risk.
- Unweaned kittens get no solid food amounts.
- Weight loss has no fixed end date.

## Cats.com

Cats.com articles are linked as further reading only. Its online weight calculator gets its result from the server, so its formula cannot be seen from the public page. Cat Compass uses the published AAHA formula instead and does not copy the site's text, photos, styles or code.
